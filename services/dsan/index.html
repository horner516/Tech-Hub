Find devices on the network

Scan up to 256 addresses and four ports. The scan listens for valid Limitimer status frames. PerfectCue may remain unknown until a button is pressed; cue bytes are labeled as a possible match. Choose a device, then Save and connect.

Some serial adapters allow only one connection; scanning may briefly occupy a connection. Already-connected devices in this app are listed without probing them.
